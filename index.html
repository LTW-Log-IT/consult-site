Who we help

Built for small businesses at every stage of federal work.

Getting started

You have a strong commercial business and want to sell to the federal government, but you are not sure where your work fits or how buyers find vendors. We help you build a target list and a plan before you spend money chasing solicitations.

Bidding but not winning yet

You are registered and responding, but proposals go out without a clear decision process and results are uneven. We bring structure: qualification, capture, and reviews before submission.

Teaming and growing

You want to sub to a prime, bring on partners, or move from subcontractor to prime. We help you find partners, define roles and workshare, and present your company clearly.

Services

Support across the full BD process.

Use one service for a single pursuit, or combine them into an ongoing BD function your team can keep running.

Opportunity identification

Research SAM.gov notices, agency forecasts, and pre-solicitation activity to build a pipeline that matches what you actually do.

Bid/no-bid qualification

Score each opportunity on fit, customer access, competition, and resources so you decide with facts before committing writers.

Capture planning

Plan the win before the RFP: customer needs, competitor view, win themes, and an action list with owners.

Teaming partners and agreements

Identify partners who close your capability gaps, sort out prime/sub roles and workshare, and prepare for teaming agreement discussions.

Proposal development

Proposal management, writing, compliance matrix, outlines, and color-team style reviews built around the evaluation criteria.

RFI and sources-sought responses

Clear, specific responses that tell the contracting officer what you can do, and help shape set-aside decisions early.

Capability statements

A one-page capability statement written for government buyers and prime contractors, with core competencies, differentiators, and company data.

Marketing materials

Company overview, teaming one-pager, and outreach messaging so every partner and buyer touchpoint says the same thing.

BD system and pipeline setup

A working pipeline with stages, decision gates, a teaming tracker, and simple reporting your team can keep up.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee contract awards?

No. Awards depend on the customer, the competition, pricing, and many factors outside anyone's control. We help you make better decisions and submit stronger, compliant responses.

We have never done federal work. Can you still help?

Yes. Many of the companies we want to help are starting out. We begin with where your work fits and which buyers to target.

Do you write the proposal or review ours?

Either. We can manage and write with your subject-matter experts, or review your draft against the instructions and evaluation criteria.

Do you need a CAGE code to work with us?

No. Our CAGE code is pending. Our delivery pursuits are subcontract/teaming until CAGE is active. Consulting sold commercially to companies does not require a CAGE code; a federal consulting task order does.

How do you handle conflicts of interest?

We run a conflict check before proposal support and keep client bid data separate from our own pursuits. Read the Firewall policy.

Have an opportunity in view, or just getting started?

Tell us about your company and what you are pursuing. We will reply with where we would start.
