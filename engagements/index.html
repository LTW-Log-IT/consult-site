Engagements

Start with the decision in front of you.

Each engagement maps to stages of the method. Scope is set after a short conversation about your opportunity and a conflict check.

Bid decision sprint

Go/no-go on one opportunity

Who it’s for
A small business or capture team looking at one specific opportunity and needing a clear decision before committing proposal resources.
What you get
A structured qualification of the opportunity against your capability, customer access, competition, teaming position, and available resources, ending in a go or no-go recommendation.
Typical output
Completed go/no-go scorecard, key risks and gaps, and a short recommendation memo.

Capture plan

Plan to win before the RFP

Who it’s for
A team that has decided to pursue and wants a disciplined plan between the go decision and the solicitation.
What you get
Customer needs, competitive view, win themes, teaming approach, and the actions and owners needed to be positioned when the RFP releases.
Typical output
Capture plan document with an action list, owners, and dates.

Proposal support or review-only

Color-team style review

Who it’s for
A team writing a response that wants hands-on proposal management help, or an independent review of a draft.
What you get
Either support organizing and managing the response (compliance matrix, outline, assignments, schedule) or a review-only engagement that evaluates your draft against the instructions and evaluation criteria.
Typical output
Compliance matrix and annotated outline, or written color-team review findings with prioritized fixes.

BD system stand-up

Pipeline, gates, dashboards, teaming tracker

Who it’s for
A company pursuing work without a repeatable BD process, or one whose pipeline lives in inboxes and spreadsheets nobody trusts.
What you get
A working BD operating system: a pipeline with defined stages, stage gates with decision criteria, simple dashboards, and a teaming tracker your team can keep running.
Typical output
Configured pipeline and stage definitions, gate criteria and scorecards, dashboard views, and a teaming tracker.

Win/loss after-action

Lessons learned you can use

Who it’s for
A team that just won or lost a bid and wants to understand why before the next one.
What you get
A structured review of the pursuit from qualification through debrief, focused on decisions and process rather than blame.
Typical output
After-action report with findings and specific changes to your criteria, templates, and gates.
