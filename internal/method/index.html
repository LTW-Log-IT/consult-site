Method

Twelve stages from strategy to delivery handoff.

Each stage has a clear purpose and a concrete output. Three gates force a decision before you spend more: go/no-go at Qualify, bid/no-bid before Proposal, and a submit review before anything goes out the door.

The three gates

Gate 1 · Stage 03

Go/no-go at Qualify

Is this opportunity worth capture effort? Decided on a scorecard, not on enthusiasm.

Gate 2 · Before stage 07

Bid/no-bid before Proposal

With the solicitation in hand, are we positioned to win? If not, stop before staffing the proposal.

Gate 3 · Stage 08

Submit review

Is the package compliant, complete, and responsive to the evaluation criteria?

  1. 01

    Strategy

    We confirm which customers, missions, and contract types fit your company, so the pipeline is built on a deliberate target list instead of whatever shows up in a search feed.

    What you get: A short target-market statement and pursuit criteria.

  2. 02

    Identify

    We find and log opportunities that match your criteria early, from forecasts, pre-solicitation notices, and customer engagement, before the RFP drops.

    What you get: A pipeline entry for each opportunity with source, timing, and fit notes.

  3. 03

    Gate: go/no-go

    Qualify (go/no-go)

    We score the opportunity against your capability, customer access, competition, teaming position, and resources. You decide go or no-go with the facts in front of you.

    What you get: A completed go/no-go scorecard and a recommendation.

  4. 04

    Capture

    We build the plan to win: customer needs, competitor view, win themes, and the actions and owners needed before the solicitation releases.

    What you get: A capture plan with an action list and owners.

  5. 05

    Teaming

    We identify the gaps your company cannot cover alone and work through prime/sub roles, partner fit, and workshare before agreements are signed.

    What you get: A teaming tracker with partner status and open items.

  6. 06

    Solution

    We shape the technical and management approach so it answers what the customer actually needs and can be staffed and delivered.

    What you get: A solution outline tied to customer requirements and win themes.

  7. 07

    Gate: bid/no-bid before proposal

    Proposal

    We organize the response: compliance matrix, outline, writing assignments, and color-team reviews, so the team writes to the evaluation criteria.

    What you get: A compliance matrix, annotated outline, and review findings.

  8. 08

    Gate: submit review

    Submit

    We run a final review against the instructions and evaluation criteria and confirm the package is complete and on time.

    What you get: A submission checklist and final review sign-off.

  9. 09

    Orals / negotiation

    If the customer calls for orals, discussions, or clarifications, we help you prepare, rehearse, and respond consistently with the proposal.

    What you get: Prep materials, rehearsal feedback, and response drafts.

  10. 10

    Award / debrief

    Win or lose, we help you request and prepare for the debrief so you capture what the evaluators saw.

    What you get: Debrief questions and a summary of findings.

  11. 11

    Lessons learned

    We run an after-action review on what worked and what did not, and feed it back into your criteria, templates, and gates.

    What you get: An after-action report with specific changes to your process.

  12. 12

    Handoff to delivery

    On a win, we hand the commitments made in the proposal to the delivery team so nothing promised gets lost at startup.

    What you get: A handoff package: commitments, key personnel, and open risks.
