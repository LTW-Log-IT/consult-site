Firewall

Conflict and data policy.

We also pursue work of our own as a teaming partner. These rules keep that separate from the work we do for you.

Client bid data is isolated

Client bid data is kept separate from Lead the Way Logistics & IT’s own teaming and delivery pursuits, with separate storage and access. It is not shared and not reused.

Conflict check before proposal support

Before any proposal support engagement, we check the opportunity against our own pursuits and against other clients on the same opportunity.

No consult-then-subcontract

We refuse to consult a prime and then subcontract to that prime on the same pursuit.

Disclose and step back

If a conflict appears mid-engagement, we disclose it and step back.

CAGE status

CAGE pending. Our delivery pursuits are subcontract/teaming until CAGE is active. Consulting sold commercially to companies does not require a CAGE code; a federal consulting task order does.
