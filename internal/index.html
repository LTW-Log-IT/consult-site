The problem

Proposals get staffed before the opportunity is qualified.

An RFP drops, the team rallies, and writers, reviewers, and partners are committed before anyone has asked whether the company can actually win. Weeks later, the proposal goes out against an incumbent the customer already prefers, with a teaming gap nobody closed.

Every no-go decision made late costs real people's time. A disciplined go/no-go up front, followed by capture and teaming work before the solicitation, is how a small business spends its BD hours on the bids it can win.
